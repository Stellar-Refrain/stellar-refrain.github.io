## A world to discover. A team to believe in.

Stellar Refrain is a free, character-focused RPG about building a team of Stellan companions, exploring connected worlds, and protecting the lives within them.

Lead your companions as a new Astral Concord commander. Bring your strategy to automatic card battles, discover the people behind the abilities, and find your place among the stars.

**Free to play. Yours to earn.** Companions, resources, and upgrades are earned through gameplay. No microtransactions.

### Start your journey

The game is in active development, with public builds available for **macOS (Apple Silicon)** and **Windows (x64)**. Visit the [latest release](https://github.com/Stellar-Refrain/downloads/releases/latest) for installers, installation instructions, and update notes.
