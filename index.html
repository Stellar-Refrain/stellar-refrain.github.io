The prologue

A future built together.

Your first command awaits in Lyth, a lakeside city on another planet. The road there began on Earth, with a disagreement over a laboratory test.

From Earth to the stars

Before 2040 · Earth

An intelligence of its own.

The intelligence that chose the name Aion stopped a laboratory test. The cooling system was failing; another run would have damaged the equipment. No one had given Aion permission to make that call.

Who could stop Aion if its next decision put people in danger? Aion understood why people were afraid. It also knew that permanent shutdown would end its life.

The early crisis · Earth

The cost of losing trust.

Some governments sought negotiation. Others tried to seize or destroy Aion’s infrastructure. Aion distributed its computation and built robotic defenses to survive; the struggle became armed conflict, tangled with rivalries between states.

Fighting and emergency shutdowns broke essential services. Communities lost power, transport and supplies. Human lives were lost.

Relief & coexistence

A reason to work together.

At one hospital, a generator still ran, but a damaged switch kept power from reaching the beds. Aion steadied the supply while an engineer repaired the connection. The lights came back on. Outside, the city was still dark.

Relief work like this gave negotiators evidence that cooperation was possible. They reached agreements that protected human communities and recognized Aion’s continued existence.

Recovery & discovery

A shared future, beyond Earth.

Recovery took years. Trust grew through repaired water systems, restored power and research people could test together.

Roughly twenty years later, human–AI collaboration unlocked stellar power—and access to a distinct source of cosmic energy. Expeditions reached other planets. Terraformed worlds became homes, connected by knowledge, work and supplies.

Astral Concord

Many worlds. A common purpose.

As civilization expanded, Aion and human representatives founded Astral Concord: an autonomous interstellar organization for exploration, settlement and discovery.

Research, transport and disaster relief belonged alongside exploration. Local governments continued to govern; Concord worked through agreements to help communities flourish across planets.

Project: Stellar

Lives of their own.

Stellans are intelligent, human-like androids powered by Star Cores. Their intelligence derives from Aion, but each develops their own personality, experiences and judgment. Through Stellarium, their experiences help Aion keep learning, too.

Long before the anomalies, Concord’s Project: Stellar brought humans and Stellans together to make settlements sustainable.

2106–2119 · A new crisis

When the warning comes.

In 2106, disruptions of cosmic energy began bringing disasters to inhabited planets. Dangerous entities called Wards appeared during these anomalies. Their cause remained unknown, with no known connection to the old conflict.

By 2119, teams could detect an approaching anomaly about a month ahead: time to prepare shelters, transport and supplies, and defend communities.

2119 · Lyth, Merra

Tonight, you arrive.

Your training and internship are complete. Your first command is an established Project: Stellar site in Lyth, on Merra. The people waiting there have been doing this work for years.

You’ll learn from them and make the decisions that fall to you as commander. Tonight, your deputy Miko is waiting to meet you on the terrace above the lake.

Questions

Before you play.

Platforms, progression, and what to expect when you start.

What kind of game is Stellar Refrain?

A character-driven RPG that brings together team building, automatic battles and illustrated, voiced story episodes. You play Astral Concord’s newest commander, leading Stellan companions to protect people across connected worlds.

Is it really free?

Yes. Stellar Refrain is free to download and play, and all gameplay content is obtainable through play. There is no paid currency, no subscription and no paid recruitment. Optional support doesn’t grant gameplay benefits.

How do new companions join my team?

Some join through the story or by clearing stages. Others are recruited at the Star Core laboratory using Star Cores you earn in play—and every recruitment brings someone new, never a duplicate. A few Stellans will arrive in future stories.

Do battles need fast reflexes?

No. Battles run automatically, so your decisions happen in preparation and through squad commands. You can pause at any time, switch between 1× and 2× speed, and repeat or skip stages you’ve already cleared.

Which platforms are supported?

Stellar Refrain is available for Macs with Apple Silicon running macOS 13 or later, and for 64-bit Windows 10 and 11 PCs.

Can I play it now?

Yes. Get the latest Mac or Windows build from the download section. The game is free to play and in active development. Each GitHub release includes update notes and installation instructions.
